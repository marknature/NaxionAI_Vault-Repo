AI-Powered Fraud Is Reshaping Risk in Africa — Are You Ready?

TransUnion’s H1 2025 Africa Fraud Update reported a 1,200% increase in deepfake‑enabled scams in South Africa, with substantial spikes also observed in Kenya, Nigeria and Algeria. Fraudsters are deploying generative AI to create fake voice notes on WhatsApp and to forge onboarding documents, making scams harder to detect. The study highlights how AI is lowering the technical barrier for cybercriminals across the continent. This surge poses rising threats to digital trust and financial security for businesses and consumers.

Why it matters
The rise in AI‑powered fraud means that traditional verification methods are increasingly vulnerable, potentially leading to higher financial losses and erosion of consumer confidence. It signals an urgent need for businesses to invest in AI‑based detection tools and for regulators to update frameworks to address synthetic media threats. Consumers also need greater awareness to spot deepfake‑based scams on everyday platforms.
