Truecaller Confirms Opt-Out Option for Nigerians Following Privacy Ruling

Truecaller has announced that Nigerian citizens can remove their phone numbers from its caller-identification database. This statement follows a Lagos High Court ruling that dismissed a privacy case brought against the company by individuals who were not registered users of the platform. The company clarified that it does not sell user contacts and provides a mechanism for any individual to unlist their details. This development underscores the legal complexities of data-driven services operating within Nigeria's evolving regulatory framework.

Why it matters for Africa
Why it matters for Africa: This case highlights the critical balance between AI-driven utility and individual data sovereignty in Africa’s largest tech market. It establishes a clear expectation for global tech platforms to provide accessible opt-out tools, ensuring that data privacy remains a priority as digital identification services expand.
