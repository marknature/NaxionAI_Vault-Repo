African Development Bank Group launches free AI training

The African Development Bank Group, in partnership with other organizations, has launched a free AI training program for African public officials. The training aims to equip officials with the skills to effectively utilize AI in governance and public service. The program is part of efforts to promote digital transformation and improve public services in Africa. The training will cover topics such as AI fundamentals, data science, and machine learning.

Why it matters for Africa
This initiative matters for Africa because it addresses the need for public officials to be equipped with the necessary skills to effectively utilize AI in governance and public service. This can lead to improved public services, increased efficiency, and better decision-making. The program also aligns with the African Development Bank's goal of promoting digital transformation in Africa.
