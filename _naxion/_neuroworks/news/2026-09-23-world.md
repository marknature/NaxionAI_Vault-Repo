Orbital Collisions Raise Alarms

A new report from Europe warns of the growing risk of orbital collisions, with over 17,000 satellites now in orbit and increasing. The Kessler syndrome, popularized by the 2013 film Gravity, could become a reality, making certain orbits unusable. The report highlights the need for better space debris management and mitigation strategies.

Why it matters
This matters because the increasing number of satellites in orbit poses a significant risk to global navigation, communication, and space exploration. If left unchecked, orbital collisions could lead to catastrophic consequences, including the loss of critical infrastructure and potentially even human life.
