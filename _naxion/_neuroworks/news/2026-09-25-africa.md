Africa's AI boom leaves 100 million disabled citizens behind

Africa's AI boom is leaving 100 million disabled citizens behind, highlighting the need for inclusive AI development and deployment in Africa. This is due to a lack of accessibility features in AI systems, making it difficult for people with disabilities to use and benefit from these technologies. The issue is particularly pressing in Africa, where people with disabilities are often marginalized and excluded from economic and social opportunities. The article highlights the need for policymakers and tech companies to prioritize inclusive AI development and deployment.

Why it matters for Africa
Why it matters for Africa is that the exclusion of people with disabilities from AI development and deployment can exacerbate existing social and economic inequalities. By prioritizing inclusive AI, Africa can promote digital inclusion and create opportunities for people with disabilities to participate in the economy and society. This is crucial for achieving the continent's development goals and promoting a more equitable and just society.
