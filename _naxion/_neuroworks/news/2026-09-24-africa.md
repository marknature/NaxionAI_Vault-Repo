Côte d'Ivoire Expands AI-Powered Health Logistics

This expansion matters for Africa as it demonstrates the potential of AI to transform healthcare delivery and access. By leveraging AI to optimize logistics, Côte d'Ivoire and Zipline can improve healthcare outcomes and save lives, particularly in rural and underserved areas.
