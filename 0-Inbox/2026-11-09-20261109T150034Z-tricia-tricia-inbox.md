## 📥 Inbox Triage & Drafting Process

### 1. Triage & Classification
* **Scope:** Define account, folders, and time window. Default to read/draft permissions only.
* **Retrieval:** Load full threads to capture all unanswered questions.
* **Categories:**
    * **Urgent Reply:** Deadlines, blockers, security, financial, or executive requests.
    * **Reply:** Standard questions/requests.
    * **Action:** Scheduling, payments, or system updates.
    * **Other:** Waiting, Reference, or Noise.

### 2. Drafting Urgent Replies
* **Voice Calibration:** Analyze 20–50 recent sent emails to mimic the user's greetings, sign-offs, and formality.
* **Execution:** Answer all material questions using thread context. State uncertainty clearly; do not invent commitments.

### 3. Flagging & Approval
* **Approval Batch:** Present a list containing recipient, action, draft summary, deadline, and risk level.
* **Verification:** Execute (send/label/archive) only after explicit user approval.
* **Confirmation:** Verify and report successful delivery via provider confirmation.
