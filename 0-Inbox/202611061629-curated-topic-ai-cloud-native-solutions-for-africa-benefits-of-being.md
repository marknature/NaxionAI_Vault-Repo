### Slide 2: AI for Africa: The Opportunity
To transition from passive consumers to active creators of technology, Africa must bridge three critical infrastructure gaps [1]:
*   **⚡ Power:** AI is energy-hungry; a revolution in energy infrastructure is required to sustain the high-compute needs of data centres [1].
*   **🌐 Connectivity:** Moving beyond basic mobile access to establish high-bandwidth, reliable backbone infrastructure for modern digital services [1].
*   **📂 Data:** Developing open, structured datasets that reflect local realities to ensure African contexts are not "invisible" to global models [1].

### Slide 3: AI-Native & Cloud-Native Solutions
Scaling AI across the continent requires the marriage of **Cloud Native** architecture (containers, microservices, serverless) with **AI-Native** application design.

| Component | Cloud Native Approach | AI-Native Benefit |

| **Deployment** | Kubernetes / Containerization | Rapid scaling of LLMs across diverse regions. |

### Slide 4: Professionalism in the Era of AGI/SI
The transition toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Super Intelligence (SI) fundamentally alters the labor market.

**⚠️ The Primary Risk: Role Displacement**
*   **Termination of Simple Roles:** Routine, repetitive, and purely administrative tasks are highly susceptible to automation.
*   **Skill Obsolescence:** Technical skills that do not evolve alongside AI (e.g., basic coding or data entry) lose market value.

**🚀 The Opportunity: New Value Creation**
*   **Creation of New Jobs:** Emergence of roles such as AI Orchestrators, Prompt Engineers, and AI Ethics Auditors.
*   **The "Human-in-the-Loop" Advantage:** Professionals who can synthesize AI output with deep domain expertise, emotional intelligence, and strategic judgment become indispensable.
*   **Strategic Pivot:** The shift from "doing the work" to "directing the intelligence."
