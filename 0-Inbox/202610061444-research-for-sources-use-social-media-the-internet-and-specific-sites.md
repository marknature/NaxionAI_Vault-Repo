# Research: For sources use social media, the internet and specific sites that post events and competitions. Then also have the scan also look for 2 global remote hackathons per scan

To discover hackathon opportunities you can combine three types of sources:

* **Social‑media pages** – groups or accounts that regularly share event posts (e.g., the Tunisia.AI Facebook group that curates international AI hackathons and competitions) [3].  
* **Event‑listing websites** – platforms that aggregate hackathons from many organizers (e.g., Devpost’s hackathon browse page) [2].  
* **Specialized hackathon hubs** – sites run by organizers or sponsors that list their own events (e.g., lablab.ai’s AI Hackathons index) [1].

From the lablab.ai listing, two **global‑remote (online) hackathons** that are open worldwide are:

1. **WeAreDevelopers x BAND present: Dark Factory (hackathon edition)** – fully online, build period Sept 26 – Oct 5 2026 [1].  
2. **AssemblyAI – Voice Agent Hackathon** – month‑long online challenge, Sept 1 – 30 2026 [1].

Both are accessible from anywhere, require only an internet connection, and are promoted on the sites mentioned above, satisfying the request to use social media, internet event sites, and to scan for two global remote hackathons per cycle.
