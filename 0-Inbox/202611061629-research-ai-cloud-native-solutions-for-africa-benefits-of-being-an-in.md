*   **Power:** AI is "energy hungry," requiring a revolution in energy infrastructure to support the computation needs of data centres [1].
*   **Connectivity:** Beyond basic mobile access, there is a need for high-bandwidth, reliable backbone infrastructure to support modern digital services [1].
