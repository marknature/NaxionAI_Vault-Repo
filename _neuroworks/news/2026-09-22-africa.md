Exit drought paralyses early-stage African tech

The number of African startups raising between $100,000 and $1 million has fallen by almost half over the past six months. This decline is attributed to a lack of funding and a decrease in the number of investors. Husein Merchant, who leads Village Capital's regional operations, notes that this trend is a concern for the growth of the African tech ecosystem. The decline in funding for early-stage startups can hinder innovation and job creation in the region.

Why it matters for Africa
This matters for Africa because a thriving tech ecosystem is crucial for economic growth and job creation. A decline in funding for early-stage startups can have long-term consequences for the region's ability to innovate and compete globally.
