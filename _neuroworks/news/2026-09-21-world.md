UN says AI safeguards can't wait for certainty

The United Nations says that safeguards for artificial intelligence cannot wait for certainty and that precautionary measures should be taken to prevent potential harm. This statement comes from a UN panel discussing AI, which highlighted the need for immediate action to address the risks associated with AI. The panel emphasized that the benefits of AI should not come at the expense of human well-being and safety.

Why it matters
This matters because the UN's stance on AI safeguards sets a precedent for international cooperation and regulation. It also highlights the need for governments and industries to take proactive measures to mitigate the risks associated with AI, rather than waiting for certainty about its consequences.
