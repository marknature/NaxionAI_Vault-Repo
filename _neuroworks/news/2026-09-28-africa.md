Kenya's Rhea Secures $100K to Scale Soil Tech into Tanzania

Kenya-based startup Rhea has secured $100,000 in funding to scale its soil technology into Tanzania. The funding will be used to expand Rhea's soil testing and analysis services to Tanzanian farmers, helping them make informed decisions about crop management and improve yields. Rhea's technology uses machine learning and IoT sensors to analyze soil health and provide personalized recommendations to farmers.

Why it matters for Africa
This development matters for Africa as it highlights the potential of African startups to drive innovation and solve real-world problems in agriculture, a critical sector for the continent's economic growth. The funding also demonstrates the growing interest in African soil health and the need for data-driven solutions to improve crop yields and reduce waste.
