Tesla's big electric truck faces infrastructure challenge

Tesla's Semi electric truck has a 500-mile range, but charging gaps still limit electric trucking. The truck's arrival coincides with ongoing infrastructure challenges, including charging gaps and limited access to high-power charging stations. This raises questions about the practicality of long-haul electric trucking. The issue highlights the need for significant investments in charging infrastructure to support widespread adoption of electric trucks.

Why it matters
The success of electric trucks depends on the availability of charging infrastructure. Without sufficient charging points, electric trucks may not be viable for long-haul routes, limiting their adoption and potential to reduce emissions.
