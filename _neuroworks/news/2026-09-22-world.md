Meta patches Muse exploit

Meta has issued a patch for its Muse macOS app after discovering a zero-day vulnerability that allowed attackers to control the AI agent. The vulnerability, which was discovered before it was exploited, could have enabled malicious actors to access sensitive information and disrupt user experiences. The patch is intended to prevent unauthorized access and maintain the security of the Muse app.

Why it matters
This patch is significant because it highlights the importance of timely security updates in preventing potential attacks on AI-powered applications. It also underscores the need for developers to prioritize security and vulnerability testing in their development processes.
