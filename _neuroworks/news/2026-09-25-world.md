Trump Admin Uses AI to Deny Medical Care for Seniors

The Trump administration is using AI to deny medical care for seniors in a disastrous experiment. Vendors rolling out AI have an incentive to deny as many claims as possible, leading to potentially life-threatening consequences for those in need. This move has been criticized as a "disgrace" by experts. The AI system is being used to process Medicare claims, with the goal of reducing costs.

Why it matters
This development raises serious concerns about the use of AI in healthcare, particularly when it comes to vulnerable populations like seniors. The potential consequences of AI-driven decision-making in healthcare are far-reaching and could have devastating effects on those who rely on these services.
